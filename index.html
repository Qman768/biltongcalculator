Ingredients

Meat

  • 1000 g Beef — silverside or topside/rump

Curing spice mix

  • 2.5 Tbsp Brown (malt) or cider vinegar
  • 1.06 Tbsp Coarse salt (~1.7% of the meat weight, reduced from original 2%)
  • 1 tsp Ground black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp Coriander seed
  • 0.75 Tbsp Brown sugar (optional)
Ratios based on Greedy Ferret's Perfect Biltong Recipe
